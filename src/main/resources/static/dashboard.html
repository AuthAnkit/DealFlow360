About this build

DealFlow360 is a self-governing sales operations platform: quotations are auto-routed for discount approval using a blended risk score, fulfillment is auto-split across warehouses based on live stock, subscriptions bill and prorate on their own schedule, customers negotiate directly in a separate restricted portal, and the Deal Health dashboard surfaces stalled deals, discount anomalies and delivery slippage in real time.
