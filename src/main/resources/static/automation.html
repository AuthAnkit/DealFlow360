Automation Activity

Added feature - DealFlow360 runs three background jobs on its own, with no user action, every few minutes. This page is the transparency window into what they've done: nothing here is a black box.

Auto-nudge stalled deals

Re-checks Deal Health on a timer and logs an automatic reminder against any deal that's gone quiet, instead of waiting for a manager to notice.

Auto-consolidate backorders

Re-checks every open backorder against current stock and fulfills whatever has since become available - the same "Consolidate" action, run for you.

Auto-flag low stock

Scans every warehouse/product stock level and flags anything at or below its replenishment threshold.
